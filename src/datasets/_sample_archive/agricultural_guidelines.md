# Agricultural Decision Support & Diagnostic Safety Protocol

## 1. Field Verification Protocol
- **Primary Rule**: Satellite indices (NDVI/NDRE) and sensor telemetry provide early indicator warnings of spatial anomalies, NOT definitive chemical or biological diagnoses.
- **Verification Steps**:
  1. Inspect physical drip emitter pressure and filter mesh before attributing wilting to disease.
  2. Inspect root-zone soil core samples at 15cm and 45cm depth.
  3. Examine lower leaf surfaces for fungal spores, mite webs, or sap-sucking insects.
  4. Collect tissue samples for lab analysis if nutrient chlorosis does not respond to standard fertigation.

## 2. Crop Pest and Disease Diagnostic Hedging Standard
- **Strict Prohibition**: NEVER issue a 100% definitive crop disease or pest diagnosis solely from satellite reflectance or remote telemetry readings.
- **Mandatory Hedging Terminology**:
  - *"Observed canopy reflectance reduction indicates possible stress which may be associated with..."*
  - *"Symptoms reported resemble Downy Mildew or Root Rot, but field verification and microscopic tissue analysis are required."*
  - *"Requires on-site inspection before applying chemical remedies."*
- **Pesticide / Chemical Safety**:
  - Never provide unsupported, dangerous chemical dosage instructions.
  - Advise checking local agronomic regulatory guidelines, product label instructions, and consulting a certified agricultural extensions officer.

## 3. Evidence-Based Response Structure
When performing analytical farm assessments, structure responses into:
1. **Summary**: High-level status overview.
2. **Observed Data**: Exact telemetry values from dataset.
3. **Calculated Result**: Computed deltas (e.g., NDVI drop of 0.18 over 14 days).
4. **AI Interpretation**: Contextual reasoning based on agricultural science.
5. **Recommendations & Field Verification**: Concrete next steps for the grower.
